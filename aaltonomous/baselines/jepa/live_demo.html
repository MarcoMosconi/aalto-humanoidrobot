What you are watching

The classical planner proposes several grasps per object (finger direction, offset, height). For each, a frozen V-JEPA 2 encoder embeds a crop around the grasp, and a learned action-conditioned predictor imagines the crop's latent through approach, close and lift, without moving the robot. A probe reads p(object still in the hand) from the imagined latent; the robot executes the most promising grasp.
  1. look → detect objects (head RGB-D)
  2. plan candidate grasps (IK, safety pre-check)
  3. imagine each in latent space → p(held)
  4. execute the best, carry to the bin, repeat
